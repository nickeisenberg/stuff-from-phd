Hi, Nick, 
\bigskip


Here is a rather complete references. I will keep maintain this file. Please it
for private use.
\bigskip


Here are some references where you find SHE naturally arises from physical
world:   
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Random polymer model. \cite{AmirCorwinQuastel11Stationary},
		\cite{Moreno14Pos}.  
	\item Karda-Parasi-Zhang equation. \cite{KPZ86}, \cite{Hairer2014RS},
		\cite{Quastel12KPZ}, \cite{GP15KPZ}.       
	\item Parabolic Anderson model: \cite{CarmonaMolchanov94}.  
	\item Intermittence:
		\cite{Zeldovich90Almighty}, \cite{BertiniCancrini94Intermittence}.
	\item Evolution of the universe: \cite{AMS94},
		\cite{BertiniEtc94Burgers}.  
\end{enumerate}
These topics are related to each other. This is a very active and
fast-developing field. 

\bigskip

As for SHE with non Lipschitz coefficients, here are several other references
that may interest you: 
\cite{DKZ19}, \cite{Mueller91Support}, \cite{FLN19}.   


\bigskip

Here is one paper that I came across a few years ago, which is related 
to your invariant measure project. You can take a look and especially 
you can learn the literature there. The paper is also in the 
folder ``Refs" \cite{BrzezniakOndrejatSeidler16Invariant}. 

More to come in the future.
