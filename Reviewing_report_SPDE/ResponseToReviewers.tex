We would like to thank the two anonymous reviewers for carefully reading our
manuscript and for providing detailed reports, which have helped us to improve
significantly the paper. We have revised our paper accordingly. Here are a list
of changes that we have made. The page and equation numbers refer to the
revised version of the manuscript.
